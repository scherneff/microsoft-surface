## Residuals
- **R1 · 360 · header crop 96.96 % vs 98 % bar.** This is the canon R2 icon policy: hamburger, search and chevron are CSS-stroke redraws of FabricMDL2Icons glyphs. This page also adds the ShoppingCart glyph, redrawn the same way (not rehosted; licensed font). Geometry and text pair 100 % (chrome-parity ✓). Out of fix iterations at 360.
- **R2 · content drift / dynamics.** The article list is a client-rendered feed: live shows 12 per page at 1440 (20 pages) and 11 per page at 360 (22 pages). The prototype keeps the 12 captured cards and hides the 12th at < 540 (no breakpoint between 540 and 1084 was probed). Pagination (pages 2–20) and the "Search for an article" field are static here. Both are a dynamics/EDS concern (query index + client fetch), not a layout defect.
- **R3 · back-to-top fade threshold.** The 100 px scroll threshold is an approximation: live is hidden at y 30 and shown by y 189.
- **R4 · instrument.** The origin unit cache `stardust/current/measure/en-us-surface-do-more-with-surface-units.json` does not depend on width. 1440 failed (off 66) while the cache held the 360 origin. After deleting the cache, the 1440 run passed. The cache is now at 1440.
- **R5 · advisory only.** Content-diff advisories are hidden-per-breakpoint duplicates (pagination numbers at xs, the "22" alternate, mobile "Home") and canon icon entries already accepted in `stardust/replica/accepted.json`. No new accepted.json entries were made.
