Declared exactly as the live sheet does: family **`SegoeUI`** (not "Segoe UI"), `local("Segoe UI")` first, live weight map 400 / 100 (light) / 200 (semilight) / 600 / 700; stack `"Segoe UI", SegoeUI, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. Deviation from the brief's literal family name/weights on purpose: declaring "Segoe UI" made every area heading a content-diff ROLE SWAP (family `Segoe UI` vs live `Segoe UI→sys`); mirroring the live @font-face gives identical resolution. Gate: every declared face loaded on both sides.
